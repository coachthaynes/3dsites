About CCBA
Why CCBA Exists

Too many capable players get overlooked simply because they have not caught up yet, and too many youth programs get weighed down by politics before a player ever gets a fair shot. CCBA was built to be different.

Clay County Basketball Academy is where players who are not the most polished yet, boys and girls both, come to get real reps, real fundamentals, and real confidence. The goal here is not to run the top team in town. The goal is to get every player ready for that tryout, whether it is a middle school team, a high school program, or an elite club down the road.

CCBA stays small and stays focused on purpose. It is coaching done right, for the players who need it most.

Coach Tenise, Director
Who This Is For
Built For Players Getting Ready, Not Players Already There

Players who already have an elite travel offer probably do not need CCBA. Players who are still building their game are exactly who this is for, boys and girls both.

Elementary School Players

Dribbling, footwork, shooting form, and confidence with the ball, before the game gets fast and the stakes get real.

Middle School Players

Getting game ready for school tryouts and for the jump into more competitive travel basketball.

Not Yet Elite, Not A Problem

This is not a tryout for the top team in town. It is the program that gets a player ready to try out for it, on their own timeline.

Please Read Before You Sign Up
This Is A Seasonal Program, Not Year Round

CCBA does not run every month of the year. Seasons launch when enough families sign up to make it worth it, so putting a name on the list below is not a formality. It is literally what tells CCBA when to open the next season.

Spring Basketball typically runs March through July, but that only happens again if enough players are signed up ahead of time.

No signups, no season. It really is that simple.
