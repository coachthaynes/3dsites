Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Elevate Her Hoops Report ("Elevate Her," "we," "us") collects through this website, how it is used, and the choices you have. Elevate Her is operated as a service for families of girls basketball players seeking recruiting exposure.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to give us, primarily through the package inquiry form on this site. That may include:

  • Player name, high school, and graduation year
  • Parent or guardian name
  • Email address and phone number
  • Any additional details you include in a message field

We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or advertising pixels on this site, and we do not collect payment information through this website. Package pricing is discussed and any payment is arranged directly with you after a conversation, not through an automated checkout on this site.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this site is used only to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and talk through package options
  • Build and maintain a player's recruiting profile once a package is purchased
  • Send season updates related to a purchased package

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Information From Minors

This site is built around girls basketball players, many of whom are minors. The inquiry form is intended to be completed by a parent or guardian on behalf of a player, not by the player directly. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child without the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a minor has submitted information without parental involvement, contact us and we will remove it.

Player Profiles And Public Information

Once a family purchases a package, the player's name, school, stats, photos, and video may be published on a player profile page or recruiting site as agreed with the family. Our player directory page lists public high school names only and links out to each school's public MaxPreps team page. We do not publish individual student roster information on that directory unless a family has purchased a package and agreed to a live profile.

Third Party Links

This site links to third party services such as MaxPreps, Hudl, and social media platforms, and to individual player profile sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third party sites. Review their own privacy policies before sharing information with them.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted to us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can ask us to review, correct, or delete information you have submitted at any time by emailing us at the address below.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to coachthaynes@gmail.com.
