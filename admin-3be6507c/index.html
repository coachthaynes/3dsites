What this dashboard does

Essential and Elite player pages are generated live from the data below — edit a player here and her page updates immediately, no rebuild needed. Illumination pages pull her data from here too, plus her live media from the Madi Visual Dashboard.

New questionnaire submissions automatically create or update a player's page, already tagged with the right Package Tier. Package inquiry and questionnaire form submissions both show up under the Forms tab.

Templates & Tiers Key

Quick reference for which players use which page design and how to tell them apart. There are three tiers now, nothing else.

  • Essential (free) — auto generated from the free questionnaire, violet/teal design (see the sample).
  • Elite ($249) — auto generated from her own uploaded photos and video, dark red/black design in her school colors.
  • Illumination ($649) — same dark red/black design as Elite, built by the Madi Visual Dashboard team instead of self uploaded, plus NIL content.

A player's tier is set on the Players tab and controls the Articles, Fulfillment, and Illumination tabs.

Aiyana Haynes and Kennedy Jeffress are Illumination tier but hand built as standalone pages rather than run through this dashboard's Elite/Illumination pipeline. Their player records have a Live URL set so the View button on the Players tab opens their real page.
