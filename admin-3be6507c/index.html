What this dashboard does

Essential and Elite player pages are generated live from the data below — edit a player here and her page updates immediately, no rebuild needed. Illumination pages pull her data from here too, plus her live media from the Visual-Dashboard.

New questionnaire submissions automatically create or update a player's page, already tagged with the right Package Tier. Package inquiry and questionnaire form submissions both show up under the Forms tab.

Templates & Tiers Key

Quick reference for which players use which page design and how to tell them apart. There are three tiers now, nothing else.

  • Essential (free) — auto generated from the free questionnaire, violet/teal design (see the sample).
  • Elite ($249) — auto generated from her own uploaded photos and video, dark red/black design in her school colors.
  • Illumination ($649) — same dark red/black design as Elite, built by the Visual-Dashboard team instead of self uploaded.

A player's tier is set on the Players tab and controls the Articles, Fulfillment, and Illumination tabs.
